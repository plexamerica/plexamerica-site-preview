01 · Why choose us

Four reasons to let us represent your brand.

Strategic border location

A strategic point in Brazil that combines a Free Trade Zone with a route to the Pacific through Bolivian territory. Your brand reaches Amazon retail and gains a Latin American route.

Structured tax advantage

We operate under the optimized tax regime of the Free Trade Zone. The result is a competitive margin that lets your brand reach consumers at market price, even against direct importers from Brazil’s South and Southeast.

An active channel in regional retail

We are not a passive cash-and-carry wholesaler — we are an active channel. We visit customers, drive sell-through, train sales staff and organize points of sale. Your brand arrives well positioned, not forgotten at the back of the shelf.

Contractual and financial discipline

Our leadership brings more than twelve years of experience in construction and supply in Northern Brazil. We apply to distribution the same contractual discipline we learned over years of operation.

02 · Our commitment

How we think about partnership.

As the saying goes, “skilled sailors make their own tide”. At PlexAmerica, we believe that strategic paths are part of sound business practice.

The internet has connected us to the most remote corners of the planet, but physical distance is still a challenge for those on opposite sides of the globe.

We enable smart business partnerships — pragmatic, focused on results and feasibility. We adapt to represent your business and to honor the commitments made between supply chains.

We open paths for those who see opportunity in every step. Count on our expertise to make your most ambitious projects viable and shorten the distance between your ideas and your commercial success.
